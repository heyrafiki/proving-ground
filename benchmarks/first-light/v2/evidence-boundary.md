# First Light Evidence Boundary

Version: 2.0.0

This pack reports exact conformance measures over a fixed 48-case fixture set. Its reference labels, review dispositions and timings are committed test inputs, which makes every result reproducible and every failure traceable to a versioned case.

English and Swahili values exercise language provenance and routing. Licensed translations, cultural adaptation and population-specific validation remain properties of each configured instrument and its reviewed clinical protocol.

Longitudinal comparison requires compatible instrument, scoring rule, language, responder role and amendment history. First Light preserves those dimensions alongside effective time and recorded time so reviewers can reconstruct the evidence available for each decision.

Instrument- and population-specific clinical evaluation publishes its own cohort design, uncertainty analysis and review record. This separation keeps conformance evidence precise while giving clinical evidence the protocol it requires.
